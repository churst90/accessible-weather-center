WeatherSTAR 3000 and Jr. Flavors

 Page updated: September 10, 2025

These are all of the known flavors observed on the WeatherSTAR 3000 and Jr. up to 2004. They are grouped by date. A flavor is a set of screens shown during a local forecast.

Dates

  • WeatherSTAR 3000 (1983)
  • WeatherSTAR 3000 (1984 - September 30, 1985)
  • WeatherSTAR 3000 (October 1, 1985 - June 29, 1986)
  • WeatherSTAR 3000 (June 29, 1986 - August 30, 1987)
  • WeatherSTAR 3000 (September 1, 1987 - December 31, 2004)
  • WeatherSTAR Jr.
